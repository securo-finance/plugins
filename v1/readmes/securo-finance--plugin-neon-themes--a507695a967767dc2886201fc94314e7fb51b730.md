# Neon themes

Four bold looks for [Securo](https://github.com/securo-finance/securo), each in light and dark:

- **Synthwave**: deep purple with hot pink and cyan
- **Matrix**: phosphor green on black, monospace text and square corners
- **Amber CRT**: amber on black, like an old terminal, in monospace
- **Cyberpunk**: electric yellow and cyan on near-black

After installing, pick one in the **Library**, on the **Themes** tab. Removing the plugin takes you back to Securo's own theme.

A theme is only colors (plus a corner radius and a font from your own device): nothing runs and nothing is downloaded. Every text color is checked for a readable contrast.

Want another look? Open an issue or a pull request.
