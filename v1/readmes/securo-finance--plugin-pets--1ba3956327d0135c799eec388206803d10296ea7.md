# Pets

A [Securo](https://github.com/securo-finance/securo) plugin with a **Pets** group and two categories:

- **Pet store & food**: Petz, Cobasi, Petlove, Petco, PetSmart, Chewy, Zooplus, Fressnapf, Maxi Zoo, Kiwoko, Tiendanimal, Arcaplanet, Pets at Home, and descriptions with "pet shop"
- **Vet**: veterinary clinics and hospitals in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian

The rules match the merchant name in the transaction description. Installing shows a preview of every category and rule before anything changes, and categories you already have aren't duplicated.

Missing a store near you? Open an issue or a pull request.
