# Pets

A [Securo](https://github.com/securo-finance/securo) plugin with a **Pets** group and four categories:

- **Pet store & food**: Petz, Cobasi, Petlove, Petland, Zee.Dog, Petco, PetSmart, Chewy, Pet Supplies Plus, Pet Valu, Zooplus, Bitiba, Fressnapf, Das Futterhaus, Maxi Zoo, Wanimo, Kiwoko, Tiendanimal, Arcaplanet, Isola dei Tesori, Pets at Home, Jollyes, and descriptions with "pet shop" or "casa de ração"
- **Vet**: veterinary clinics and animal hospitals in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian, plus Banfield and VCA
- **Grooming & care**: grooming, daycare, pet sitting and training, such as "banho e tosa", DogHero and Rover
- **Pet insurance**: pet health plans and insurance, such as Petlove Saúde, Petplan, Trupanion, Healthy Paws, Figo, Embrace, Agria and SantéVet

The rules match the merchant name in the transaction description. Installing shows a preview of every category and rule before anything changes, and categories you already have aren't duplicated. Transactions keep the categories they already have; the rules apply to new ones.

Category names are translated into English, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Spanish, French, German and Italian.

Missing a store near you? Open an issue or a pull request.
